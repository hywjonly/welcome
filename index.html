Research interests

My research interests lie in discrete geometry, a little bit about computational geometry, a little bit about functional analysis (topics on convex cones or relates to polytopes) and a little bit about theoretical computer science. In discrete geometry, I mostly work on polytopes. Topics about polytopes that I have been focusing on and have made progress are: the decomposability of polytopes and f-vectors of polytopes.

Publications

Lower bound results for conditionally decomposable polytopes (joint work with David Yost) arXiv
[Description. Suppose we have two polytopes which are combinatorially equivalent, but if one is decomposable (P=P1 Minkowski sum P2, where P1, P2 are not homothetic to P), the other one is indecomposable, then we say they are conditionally decomposable. We're interested in giving the existence of conditionally decomposable polytopes in higher dimensions, and also the minimum number of vertices of a conditionally decomposable polytope. While the latter is not achieved, we narrowed it down to [3d-3, 4d-4]. I enjoyed the discovery of this paper a lot.]

Polytopes with low excess degree (joint work with David Yost and Guillermo Pineda-Villavicencio).
[Description. Consider a d-dimensional polytope (in short, d-polytope) P, a vertex v is simple if there are exactly d edges containing it. The excess degree of a vertex is the number of "extra" edges of it, i.e. the degree of v minus d. The excess degree of a polytope is the sum of the excess degree of all its vertices. It is known that d-polytopes with excess degree in the range [1, d-3] don't exist. We push it further that d-polytopes with excess degree in the range [d+3, 2d-7] don't exist.]

A refined lower bound theorem for d-polytopes with at most 2d vertices (joint work with David Yost and Guillermo Pineda-Villavicencio).
[Description. The f-vectors of d-polytopes with exactly d+2 facets are known. We give the lower bound on the number of k-faces of a d-polytope with d+s (2 ≤ s ≤ d) vertices and with at least d+3 facets. This result serves as an important preliminary result for the paper "A lower bound theorem for d-polytopes with at most 3d-1 vertices" below.]

A lower bound theorem for d-polytopes with 2d+2 vertices (joint work with Guillermo Pineda-Villavicencio, Aholiab Tritama and David Yost).
[Description. As the title suggests, we give the minimum number of faces of d-polytopes with 2d+2 vertices. My contribution to this paper is minor, mainly in the equality analysis on the facets.]

A lower bound theorem for d-polytopes with at most 3d-1 vertices (joint work with Guillermo Pineda-Villavicencio) .
[Description. The lower bound on the number of k-faces of a d-polytope with d+s (2 ≤ s ≤ d) vertices is known, this is Grünbaum's lower bound conjecture, which was proved by Xue. We give the lower bound on the number of k-faces of a d-polytope with 2d+s (1 ≤ s ≤ d-1) vertices. This paper is lengthy.]

Characterization of decomposable d-polytopes with 2d+2, 2d+3 vertices, where d ≥ 4, d ≥ 5, respectively (joint work with David Yost) in preparation, to appear on arXiv.
